Structured Curriculum

A clear 6-month progressive roadmap from fundamentals to match-simulation and decision-making.

6-Month Comprehensive Curriculum

Phased progression that builds technique, control, and match awareness.

Month 1

Foundation & Safety

Stance, Forward Defense, High Arm Action, Basic Catching.

Month 2

Mastering the Straight Bat

Straight drives, run-up momentum, throwing technique.

Month 3

Control & Consistency

On-drive, back-foot punch, line/length bowling, close catching.

Month 4

Versatility & Cross Bat

Cut shot, sweep, basic swing/seam bowling, high catches.

Month 5

Power and Elevation

Pull shot, lofted shots, pace generation, relay throwing.

Month 6

Integration & Simulation

Game decision-making, consistency under pressure, match drills.
